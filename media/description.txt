A sleeper train window for your Pebble. Watch the carriage view shift from daylight to dusk to night. When fresh weather reports rain or thunderstorms, rain streaks the glass.

• Above the window, see the lighting phase and the latest current conditions.
• Below the clock, see the next hourly forecast and its local time.
• Choose Auto or preview Day, Dusk, Night, and Rain in settings.
• Follow your watch preference or choose 12-hour or 24-hour time.
• Show an optional date in ISO, US, or European format.

Made for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2. Configure in the Pebble app. Weather uses your connected phone’s approximate location and Open-Meteo. Time and time-based scenes work offline. The animated preview cycles through sample conditions.
